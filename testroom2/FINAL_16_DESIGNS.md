### D) Resource Interaction Audit

**Sacred Fire:**
- Generators: Lucy (DC-Legendary), Simon (FV), Ashley (FV), Sable (VA), Captain James (DC), Gom Gom Gom (DC), Garrick (DC), Castle Gardener (DC), Roger (FV), Granny (RH), Valkin (DC)
- Spenders/Payoffs: Tyler (FV, x2 damage), Mallow (FV, healing), Fed & Hayden (VA, infinite), Lucy's Shadow (DC, amplifier)
- **Verdict:** Strong generation, decent payoffs. The DC fire economy is DEEP.

**Ice Shards:**
- Generators: Sad Sal (FV), Chad (FV), Spockles (FV), Gary (FV), Zain (VA), Artemis (RH), Wise Al (RH), Powder (FV), Valkin (DC), Young Cap (RH)
- Spenders/Payoffs: Skylar (FV, +2 per shard), Eloise (FV, HP swap), Zain (VA, Ice Blade forge)
- **Verdict:** Heavy generation (mostly FV), but payoffs are thin. Only Skylar truly scales with shard count. NEEDS a non-FV payoff card.

**Burn:**
- Generators: Dylan (RH), Humar (VA), Lars (DC), Rascals (DC)
- Spenders/Payoffs: Mable Stadango (RH, spend 1 = remove 1 die + gain Sacred Fire)
- Defenders: Rook (VA, immune), Mike (RH, protects sideline)
- **Verdict:** Only 4 generators and 1 payoff. Burn is UNDERDEVELOPED. Needs at least 1 more generator and 1 more payoff.

**Magic Fireflies:**
- Generators: Bo (Set 1), Jimmy (RH), Chester (RH), Laura (DC), Goob Party (RH), Wendy (DC)
- Payoffs: The picker UI (spend to pick which ghost enters from opponent's sideline)
- **Verdict:** Good generation, but the ONLY payoff is the picker. Could use a secondary payoff.

**Moonstones:**
- Generators: Happy Crystal (VA), Natalia (VA), Harvey (DC), Gordok (RH), Granny (RH), Valkin (DC)
- Spenders/Payoffs: Benjamin (VA, free use), Bigsby (DC, summons Doom), Professor Hawking (RH, +2 dice), Eloise (FV, HP swap via Ice Shard)
- **Verdict:** Balanced. The Bigsby->Doom summon is wild. Prof Hawking rewards holding. Good.

**Lucky Stones:**
- Generators: Hank (VA), Maximo (RH), Selene (RH), Granny (RH), Jimmy (RH), Boopies (RH), Zippa (RH), Lars (DC), Wendy (DC), Valkin (DC)
- Spenders/Payoffs: Twyla (RH, +1 die + Healing Seed per stone spent)
- **Verdict:** MASSIVE generation, almost exclusively in RH. Only Twyla as a real payoff. Needs a non-RH payoff or spender.

**Healing Seeds:**
- Generators: Maximo (RH), Selene (RH), Kaplan (RH), Farmer Jeff (RH), Chester (RH), Nyx & Bessie (RH), Twyla (RH), Calvin (RH), Aunt Susan (RH), Valkin (DC)
- Spenders/Payoffs: Harrison (RH, extra dice), Chow (DC, +2 dice), Young Cap (RH, +die/Ice/Surge), Castle Gardener (DC, -> Sacred Fire), Aunt Susan (RH, +2 dmg or +2 HP), Boopies (RH, triggers Lucky Stones), Zippa (RH, Lucky Stones per seed held)
- **Verdict:** Extremely deep in RH. Well-designed spend loop. Castle Gardener bridges to DC fire.

**Surge:**
- Generators: Dart (VA), Tweak and Twonk (RH), Lars (DC), Chagrin (VA), Champ (DC), Young Cap (RH)
- Spenders: Rook (VA, +1 damage per Surge), Boris (VA, +2 HP per Surge spent)
- **Verdict:** THIN generation. Only 6 generators. Only 2 payoffs. Surge economy needs help.

### E) Archetype Team Analysis

**Burn Team:** Dylan + Humar + Rascals + Lars (generators) -> Mable Stadango (payoff)
- Missing: A SECOND burn payoff that does something different. Also missing a sideline Burn generator.

**Firefly Team:** Bo + Jimmy + Chester + Goob Party + Laura + Wendy (generators) -> Picker UI
- Missing: A card that benefits from HOLDING fireflies rather than spending them.

**Resurrection Team:** Bo (kill -> revive) + Lucas (charges revived ghosts) + ...
- Missing: A second resurrection source that doesn't require a kill.

**Blade Team:** Zain (forges Ice Blade) + Finn (forges Flame Blade) + ...
- Missing: A card that USES blades for extra effects, or a third blade type.

**Dice Manipulation Team:** Haywire (permanent +1 die) + Pip (triples = opponent -1 die) + Antoinette (mirror) + Tommy Salami (chain 6s)
- Missing: A card that CAPITALIZES on having many dice (like: "deal +1 damage per die you rolled")

**Tank Team:** Bouril (10HP), Nerina (9HP), Bubble Boys (9HP), Mountain King (9HP), Mother Nature (8HP shelved)
- Missing: A tank that rewards being HIT rather than just surviving.

**Disruption Team:** Nick & Knack + Gordok + Dallas (die theft) + Suspicious Jeff (die theft)
- Missing: A VA or DC disruption card that steals resources (not dice).

### F) "I Wish I Had" Test
1. "I wish I had a ghost that made Burn from the sideline WITHOUT being Lucy" -- sideline Burn generator
2. "I wish I had a tank that couldn't be burned" -- Rook covers Sacred Fire/Burn immunity, but no general "immune to all resource damage" tank
3. "I wish I had something that made ties GOOD" -- Jimmy and Tweak and Twonk exist, but nothing that WINS on a tie
4. "I wish there was a ghost that got stronger every round" -- scaling per-round mechanic (not round-count dependent like Dusk)
5. "I wish I had a card that punished the opponent for HAVING too many resources" -- anti-hoarding
6. "I wish there was something that let me choose WHICH die to keep after rolling" -- pick-best-dice mechanic
7. "I wish Cosmic Alignment (quads = instant win) existed" -- the unassigned mechanic
8. "I wish I had something that turned my losses into a massive finisher" -- loss-counting payoff

## Step 3: THE FINAL 16 CARDS

---

### Card 1: COSMIC JESTER
- **Set:** Dark Castle
- **Rarity:** Ghost-Rare
- **HP:** 4
- **Ability Name:** Cosmic Alignment
- **Ability Text:** "Roll quadruples (four of a kind): instantly win the game."
- **Category:** Destruction/Instant Kill
- **Why:** THE unassigned mechanic that needs a home. 4 HP ghost-rare — fragile, so you have to keep it alive long enough to get 4+ dice and land quads. With 4 dice, quads are ~0.46%. With 5 dice (Haywire/Tommy Salami builds), it becomes ~2.3%. The dream. Every roll with this card active makes the whole room hold their breath. Dark Castle because it's theatrical, dramatic, impossible — exactly their energy.

---

### Card 2: EMBER JENNY
- **Set:** Volcanic Isles
- **Rarity:** Common
- **HP:** 3
- **Ability Name:** Campfire
- **Ability Text:** "Sideline & In Play: at the end of each round, gain 1 Burn."
- **Category:** Resource Generation
- **Why:** Fills the "sideline Burn generator" gap. No combat required — just existing produces Burn. 3 HP fragile but fires from sideline. Feeds Mable Stadango's Hex without requiring wins. VA needs commons desperately (only 4). Simple, elegant, thematic — a little ember spirit tending a campfire.

---

### Card 3: SIREN
- **Set:** Frost Valley
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **HP:** 5
- **Ability Name:** Frozen Crescendo
- **Ability Text:** "Win: spend any number of Ice Shards. Each shard spent deals +2 damage this roll."
- **Category:** Damage Multiplier
- **Why:** The missing non-Skylar Ice Shard payoff. Skylar makes shards deal +2 passively; Siren lets you DUMP them all at once for a massive hit. 5 Ice Shards = +10 bonus damage on a single winning roll. The "I've been saving up for THIS" moment. Frost Valley rare — completes the Ice Shard spend archetype alongside Eloise (HP swap) and Zain (Ice Blade).

---

### Card 4: FLICKER
- **Set:** Volcanic Isles
- **Rarity:** Common
- **HP:** 4
- **Ability Name:** Tantrum
- **Ability Text:** "Lose a roll: gain 2 Surge."
- **Category:** Resource Generation
- **Why:** VA needs commons AND Surge needs generators. Chagrin does 1 Surge on loss (VA rare). Flicker does 2 on loss at common rarity with 4 HP — a reliable Surge farm for budget decks. Pairs with Rook (Surge -> damage) and Boris (Surge -> HP). The angry little flame that gets stronger when frustrated.

---

### Card 5: NIGHTINGALE
- **Set:** Dark Castle
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **HP:** 5
- **Ability Name:** Requiem
- **Ability Text:** "Sideline: when one of your ghosts is defeated, your next ghost enters with +2 dice on its first roll."
- **Category:** Prediction/Reaction
- **Why:** DC has 0 "death payoff" cards and barely any Prediction/Reaction. Nightingale sings a requiem from the sideline — the next ghost enters battle furious and empowered. Not a resurrection, not healing — a momentum transfer. Makes losing a ghost feel less hopeless. 5 HP uncommon is solid if forced to fight.

---

### Card 6: GRUDGE
- **Set:** Volcanic Isles
- **Rarity:** Common
- **HP:** 5
- **Ability Name:** Slow Boil
- **Ability Text:** "Each round you lose: gain +1 permanent damage for the rest of the game."
- **Category:** Damage Multiplier
- **Why:** The "I wish there was a ghost that got stronger every round" and "loss-stacking revenge card." Every loss makes Grudge angrier. After 3 losses: every hit deals +3. The impossible comeback card. 5 HP common — survives long enough to stack. VA common (filling the gap). Permanent effect is allowed per Haywire/Pip precedent.

---

### Card 7: VAULT
- **Set:** Dark Castle
- **Rarity:** Common
- **HP:** 6
- **Ability Name:** Lockbox
- **Ability Text:** "Sideline: your resources cannot be stolen or discarded by opponent effects."
- **Category:** Defense/Negation
- **Why:** Pure anti-disruption. Counters Nick & Knack, Gordok, and any future resource theft. 6 HP common tank that protects your economy from the sideline. DC needs commons badly. Simple, defensive, satisfying for the player who just got robbed three games in a row.

---

### Card 8: TORCHBEARER
- **Set:** Volcanic Isles
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **HP:** 5
- **Ability Name:** Rally Cry
- **Ability Text:** "Win: gain 1 Surge. If you have 3+ Surge when you win, deal +4 damage and discard all Surge."
- **Category:** Damage Multiplier
- **Why:** Surge economy desperately needs a card that both generates AND has a built-in payoff in the same card. Torchbearer self-fuels: win 3 times -> 3 Surge -> 4th win explodes for +4. Then restarts. VA uncommon filling the gap. The rhythm card — build, build, build, BOOM.

---

### Card 9: UNDERTOW
- **Set:** Frost Valley
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **HP:** 6
- **Ability Name:** Riptide
- **Ability Text:** "Before rolling: you may spend 2 Lucky Stones to remove 1 opponent die this roll."
- **Category:** Dice Modifier
- **Why:** Lucky Stones have MASSIVE generation (10+ cards) but only Twyla as a payoff. Undertow gives a completely different spend path — offensive die removal. 2 stones = -1 enemy die is fair (Lucky Stones are easy to generate). FV uncommon — ice/water theme. Non-RH Lucky Stone spender, which is exactly what the audit identified as missing.

---

### Card 10: PHOENIX WRIGHT
- **Set:** Dark Castle
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **HP:** 3
- **Ability Name:** Objection
- **Ability Text:** "Before rolling: name a number 1-6. If your opponent's highest die equals that number, negate all their damage and deal 3 to them."
- **Category:** Prediction/Reaction
- **Why:** DC has almost no Prediction/Reaction cards. This is the ultimate prediction card — call the exact number, get rewarded with a full reversal. 1-in-6 per die, but with 3 opponent dice that's ~42% of at least one matching. The "CALLED IT!" moment. 3 HP glass cannon — lives or dies by the prediction. Named for the courtroom drama energy.

---

### Card 11: PYRE
- **Set:** Volcanic Isles  
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **HP:** 4
- **Ability Name:** Fuel the Flames
- **Ability Text:** "Spend 2 Burn: deal 3 damage to the enemy active ghost immediately. No roll needed."
- **Category:** Disruption
- **Why:** The SECOND Burn payoff the economy desperately needs. Mable Stadango spends Burn to remove dice; Pyre spends Burn for guaranteed direct damage. Two totally different spend paths for the same resource. 2 Burn = 3 guaranteed damage is strong but fair (Burn is hard to generate). VA finally gets a Disruption card (had 0).

---

### Card 12: BELLE
- **Set:** Dark Castle
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **HP:** 5
- **Ability Name:** Enchantment
- **Ability Text:** "While active: ties deal 3 damage to the opponent instead of 0."
- **Category:** Damage Multiplier
- **Why:** The "I wish ties were GOOD" card. Ties are ~13% — rare but with Belle active, every tie becomes a free 3 damage hit. Completely changes the emotional register of ties. DC uncommon — the enchantress of the castle who makes nothing into something. Pairs with Jimmy/Tweak and Twonk builds that want ties.

---

### Card 13: REMNANT
- **Set:** Volcanic Isles
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **HP:** 6
- **Ability Name:** Afterlife
- **Ability Text:** "When defeated: choose one of your sideline ghosts. It gains +3 HP and +1 die permanently."
- **Category:** HP Recovery
- **Why:** VA has 0 HP Recovery cards (active). Remnant provides it through death — sacrifice Remnant, empower a teammate. Not healing in the traditional sense (no per-turn sustain that breaks the game). A one-shot death trigger that creates a powerful successor. Fills HP Recovery for VA at rare tier.

---

### Card 14: MIRAGE
- **Set:** Dark Castle
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **HP:** 4
- **Ability Name:** Distortion
- **Ability Text:** "Before rolling: opponent must roll their dice one at a time. After each die, you may say 'stop' — remaining dice aren't rolled."
- **Category:** Disruption
- **Why:** DC needs Disruption (only has 1). This creates an INCREDIBLE interactive moment — watching each die land one by one, deciding when to cut them off. The psychological warfare card. "They rolled a 6... do I stop them now or risk it?" Pure Disney World "the reveal IS the gameplay" energy. 4 HP rare because it's a premium experience card.

---

### Card 15: DRIFTWOOD
- **Set:** Rolling Hills
- **Rarity:** Ghost-Rare
- **HP:** 6
- **Ability Name:** Downstream
- **Ability Text:** "Sideline & In Play: whenever any player gains a resource, you also gain 1 Surge."
- **Category:** Resource Generation
- **Why:** RH needs ghost-rares (only 2). Driftwood is the passive Surge engine that's been missing — every single resource gain by either player also gives you 1 Surge. In a resource-heavy meta, Driftwood generates Surge rapidly. Feeds Rook, Boris, Torchbearer. 6 HP ghost-rare is solid. The lazy river spirit that catches everything floating by.

---

### Card 16: STARDUST
- **Set:** Rolling Hills
- **Rarity:** Ghost-Rare
- **HP:** 5
- **Ability Name:** Wishing Hour
- **Ability Text:** "Sideline: if you hold 5+ Magic Fireflies, you may spend all of them to revive a defeated ghost with full HP."
- **Category:** HP Recovery
- **Why:** RH needs ghost-rares. Magic Fireflies need a SECOND payoff beyond the picker UI. Stardust gives Fireflies a resurrection use — spend 5 to bring someone back at full HP. Incredible late-game swing. Completes the Firefly economy as a real archetype (generate with Bo/Jimmy/Chester/Goob Party, spend on picker OR resurrection). The "OH WOW" card.

## Design Philosophy Notes

Every card above was designed to:
1. **Fill a specific gap** identified in the audit — no vanity picks
2. **Create a MOMENT** — Cosmic Jester's quad roll, Mirage's one-die-at-a-time, Phoenix Wright's "CALLED IT"
3. **Complete an archetype** — Siren finishes Ice Shard spending, Pyre finishes Burn spending, Stardust finishes Firefly spending
4. **Respect the rules** — no carryover tracking (Grudge is permanent like Haywire), no death-rattle healing, healing kept rare (only Stardust as a conditional resurrection requiring 5 Fireflies)
5. **Disney World bar** — every single one should make a kid say "I WANT TO PLAY THAT"

### Wild Card 24: PARADOX
- **Set:** Dark Castle
- **Rarity:** Ghost-Rare
- **HP:** 4
- **Ability Name:** Temporal Loop
- **Ability Text:** "Lose a roll: the round is replayed (both players re-roll). This can only trigger once per round."

- **Why it's interesting:** You lost? No you didn't — do it again. Paradox gets a SECOND CHANCE every single round it loses. The opponent could still win the replay! But the odds shift. Once per round so it can't loop infinitely. 4 HP ghost-rare — fragile but that re-roll is elite. DC because time-bending is peak castle-mystery energy.
- **Midjourney art prompt:**
