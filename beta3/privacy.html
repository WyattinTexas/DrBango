STARSPELL · Privacy Policy

Corkscrew Games · effective September 10, 2026

The short version: STARSPELL has no accounts, no sign-in, and asks for no personal information. There are no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The game keeps a small amount of pseudonymous play data so that leaderboards, duels and friends work.

What the game stores

  • A random player ID — a string of letters and digits minted on your device the first time you play. It is not your name, email, or any Apple identifier. On iPhone it is kept in the device Keychain so your progress survives a reinstall.
  • Your chosen mage name — the display name you type. Other players see it on leaderboards and in duels.
  • Game records — runs played, victories, words cast, best scores, rating, and streaks, kept so the leaderboards and your profile can show them.
  • Presence — whether you are at play right now, so friends can find you for a duel. It clears when you leave.
  • Duel records — who you dueled, when, and the dictionary words cast in the duel.
  • A small technical report — your device and browser model and a few rendering measurements, used only to keep the game rendering sharply on real phones.

What the game does not collect

No real name, no email address, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone, and no advertising identifiers. STARSPELL shows no ads, includes no analytics or advertising companies, sells no data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it lives

Play data is stored with Google Firebase, which acts as our service provider. It is used to run the game and for nothing else.

Children

STARSPELL is playable without providing any personal information. Everything described above is pseudonymous — tied to the random player ID, never to a real identity.

Deleting your data

Email wyatt@corkscrewgames.com with your mage name and we will remove your player ID and every record tied to it.
