My progress, after a reinstall

On iPhone your player identity is kept in the device Keychain, so deleting and reinstalling the app keeps your name, rating and records. They return on their own the first time you play.

Changing your mage name

Open your profile from the meadow and tap your name. Names are unique — if a name is taken, the sky will say so.

Duels

Duels are turn-based: cast your three words in your own time, and your rival answers in theirs. A standing duel waits under VERSUS until one of you finishes it.

Reporting a player name

If a name you meet in the sky is inappropriate, email us and we will deal with it.

Updates

The night sky is served fresh — the game updates itself without an App Store update.
