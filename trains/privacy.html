TRAIN GALAXY — PRIVACY POLICY

Corkscrew Games · Effective August 5, 2026

Train Galaxy ("the game") is made by Corkscrew Games. This policy covers the game at drbango.com/trains and its iOS and other app editions, which all play the same game.

We collect nothing

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, or any device identifier, and it never sends your information anywhere — the game's only network traffic is downloading the game page itself.

Saves stay on your device

Your railroad — track, stars, campaign progress, and everything bought in THE STORE — is stored locally on your device (or in your browser). It never leaves it. Clearing site data for drbango.com, or deleting the app, erases the save and starts a fresh rug.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases and nothing that costs money. Stars are earned by playing and spent in the in-game store.

Children

The game is made to be safe for children: it collects no personal information from anyone, and there is no chat, no user accounts, and no way to enter personal details anywhere in the game.
