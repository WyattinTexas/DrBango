TRAIN GALAXY — SUPPORT

Corkscrew Games

Train Galaxy is a toy train sandbox: drag wooden track onto the bedroom rug, couple up the red puffer, and press GO. Play at drbango.com/trains or in the app editions.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy. Your railroad saves on your device automatically.

Where did my railroad go? Saves live on the device (or browser) where you built them. Clearing site data — or deleting the app — starts a fresh rug.

How do I earn stars? Run trains on the PLAY rug (crashes count), and clear the ten little lines in CAMPAIGN. Spend stars in THE STORE on liveries, whistles, and crash styles.

The train won't move. Make sure the track is connected in a loop or ends at a buffer, then press GO. In CAMPAIGN, build what the ticket asks for first.

It says END OF THE LINE. The game needs an internet connection to start. Reconnect and tap TRY AGAIN.
