Notes for the Pilates Professional

  • Wyatt is 35, advanced fitness, extremely fast feet. Primary deficit is stroke power — not arm strength, but kinetic chain efficiency.
  • Power comes from: foot plant → hip load → trunk rotation → shoulder → wrist snap. Train this chain, not isolated muscles.
  • He will want to go fast — force him to slow the eccentrics. His speed is elite; his body needs to learn to LOAD before EXPLODING.
  • Watch for upper trap dominance in all arm/shoulder work — cue "shoulders away from ears" relentlessly.
  • Monitor right shoulder (paddle arm) for impingement signs.
  • Hip flexor tightness will be the biggest mobility limiter — prioritize Eve's lunge and pigeon every session.
  • Custom variations (scooter with rotation, Russian splits with chop) combine the base exercise shown in the video with the rotation/chop described in the instructions above.

Shoulder Stability & Paddle Endurance

Wednesday · 40 minutes · 9 exercises
Build the shoulder girdle to hold the paddle out front for an entire match without fatigue, plus wrist/forearm endurance for spin generation.
1

Scapular Stability & Shoulder Endurance

15 min
1
Arms in Straps: Serve Sequence 1 Green
  • Lying supine, arms in straps
  • 10 chest press → 10 arm circles → 10 T-pulls → 10 tricep press overhead
  • NO REST between movements — straight through
Mimics: maintaining paddle position through long rallies without dropping the elbow
Cue: Keep scapulae anchored to the carriage — if they wing, the spring is too heavy
2
Prone Pulling on Long Box 1 Blue
  • Lie face down on long box, arms in straps hanging toward floor
  • Pull straps to hips (back extension + retraction), hold 2 sec at top — 12 reps
Mimics: the posture and upper back engagement of the "ready position"
Cue: Pull with the lats and rhomboids, not the upper traps
3
Kneeling Chest Expansion with Head Turns 2 Red
  • Kneeling on carriage facing the ropes, pull handles behind hips
  • Hold, turn head left, center, right, center, release — 8 reps
Mimics: tracking the ball while maintaining an open chest and engaged back
4
Up-Stretch Combo 1 Red + 1 Blue
  • Pike position (hands on footbar, feet on carriage)
  • Push out to plank → pull back to pike → drop into up-stretch (chest through arms) — 8 reps
Mimics: the overhead reach pattern — thoracic extension under load
Cue: The chest-through position should feel like loading a serve
2

Wrist, Forearm & Grip

10 min
5
Wrist Circles in Straps 1 Yellow
  • Wrists in straps (wrap around wrists, not hands)
  • Flexion/extension × 15, radial/ulnar deviation × 15, circles × 10 each direction
Mimics: spin generation — topspin and slice both require wrist snap endurance
Cue: Isolate the wrist, don't let the forearm compensate
6
Forearm Plank with Perturbation Springs Off
  • Forearms on carriage, toes on platform
  • Partner gently nudges the carriage side to side while you stabilize
  • Hold 30 sec × 3 sets
Mimics: absorbing a hard drive at the kitchen — receiving pace without losing paddle control
Cue: Grip with the fingers, engage the forearm, don't hold your breath

Recovery, Mobility & Movement Quality

Saturday · 35 minutes · 11 exercises
Undo the week's accumulated tension, restore range of motion, and refine proprioception. This is where smooth movers separate from choppy ones.
1

Hip Complex

12 min
1
Frog on Carriage 1 Green
  • Prone on carriage, knees wide, heels together
  • Press out slowly (5-second eccentric), return — 10 reps
Opens the adductors and hip rotators that get tight from lateral shuffling
2
Eve's Lunge (Deep) 1 Red + 1 Blue
  • One foot on headrest, back knee on carriage, hands on footbar
  • Press carriage back into deep hip flexor stretch, hold 30 sec
  • Add rotation: rotate toward the front leg, hold 15 sec — 2 rounds each side
Hip flexor length is critical — tight hip flexors kill hip rotation which kills stroke power
3
Pigeon Stretch on Carriage 1 Blue
  • Front shin on carriage, back leg extended on platform
  • Let the carriage drift to find the stretch, hold 45 sec each side
Deep external rotator release — protects the hip joint during explosive direction changes
4
Kneeling Inner Thigh Stretch 1 Green
  • Kneel on carriage, one knee out to side on platform
  • Let carriage drift open, hold 30 sec each side
Adductor length for wide stance at the kitchen
2

Spinal Mobility

10 min
5
Mermaid (Full Expression) 1 Blue
  • Seated side-saddle on carriage, push out with inside hand on footbar
  • Top arm reaches over in full side bend, hold 5 sec — 6 each side
  • Add rotation: turn chest toward the floor at end range
Lateral trunk mobility + oblique engagement — prevents rib cage stiffness
6
Seated Spine Twist with Strap 1 Green
  • Seated on carriage, legs extended, one hand in strap
  • Rotate and pull the strap across the body, return with control — 8 each side
Active rotation through range — maintains rotational freedom built on Monday
7
Corkscrew on Long Box No Springs
  • Supine on box, legs to ceiling
  • Circle legs together — right, down, left, up — keeping pelvis stable
  • 5 each direction
Deep core stabilization — hold court position while swinging
3

Proprioception & Foot/Ankle

13 min
8
Standing Balance Series on Carriage 1 Blue
  • Stand on carriage with one foot, other foot on platform
  • Eyes open: hold 30 sec each side — Eyes closed: hold 20 sec each
  • Add micro-squats (quarter depth) on the standing leg × 8 each side
Ankle proprioception — if your ankles are smart, your feet are fast
9
Toe-Ball-Heel Presses on Footbar 2 Red
  • Supine, feet on footbar
  • Full foot articulation: toes, then ball, then full foot, then heels only — 5 reps each
Trains intrinsic foot muscles for grip and push-off on court surfaces
10
Running in Place on Carriage 2 Red
  • Supine, balls of feet on footbar
  • Alternating calf raises — 30 reps total, slow and controlled
Calf endurance and Achilles tendon health — the #2 injury site for pickleball players
11
Single-Leg Bridge on Carriage 1 Red
  • Supine on carriage, feet on platform, lift one leg
  • Press hips up and hold 5 sec, lower with control — 8 each side
Glute activation and pelvic stability — foundation of every lateral push
