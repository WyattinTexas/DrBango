💎 Specials

Moonstone (after rolling): Change ONE die to any value (1-6). Once per turn.
Lucky Stone (after rolling): Discard to reroll one die.
Surge (before rolling): Versatile energy resource. Spend to power ghost abilities.
Healing Seed (before rolling): Heal 1 HP. Cannot exceed max HP.
Ice Shard (before rolling): Adds +1 damage to a winning roll. Stacks across shards.
Sacred Fire (before rolling): Adds +3 damage to a winning roll. Stacks across fires.
Burn 🔥 — Place on enemy sideline ghosts before rolling. When the burned ghost enters play, it takes damage equal to its burn count. Stacks. Consumed on entry.

⚔️ Items

Permanent craftable equipment — toggle on/off each round before rolling.

Ice Blade — Crafted by Zain (discard 1 Ice Shard + 1 Moonstone). Once forged, toggle before rolling: +1 die and +2 damage on wins when swinging. Permanent — never consumed. One per game.
Flame Blade — Crafted by Finn (discard 2 Healing Seeds + 1 Sacred Fire). Once forged, toggle before rolling: +1 die and +5 Burn on wins when swinging. Permanent — never consumed. One per game.

✨ Magic Fireflies

Magic Fireflies ✨ — Generated by Bo's Miracle (3 on resurrection). Before rolling, trade 1 Firefly for any resource of your choice (Sacred Fire, Ice Shard, Lucky Stone, Moonstone, Healing Seed, Surge, or Burn). The ultimate wildcard resource.
