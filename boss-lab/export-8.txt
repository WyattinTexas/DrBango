NOTES: compared to the others up to this point, this is a low-economy fight. nothing wrong with that, but with the current balancing, the pace is very consistent the whole way through as we're mostly depending on the income from the TB, and that isn't necessarily interesting. that also can be a bad thing if the player doesn't get the ball rolling efficiently. adding some ways the battle can ebb and flow would not only make it more engaging, but would also give the player the opportunity to recover from failure or press the advantage if they're doing well, leading to a satisfying snowball effect.

the adds are noticeably more dangerous. if they were worth a substantial bounty of dog tags - not the bandits, but the special enemies like the lady frogs and Croak's son - it would give the player those opportunities. there should also be some early fast adds just to pressure the player, otherwise there's no sense in deploying units until some bandits show up to threaten the TB...

the other problem though is that a critical mass of stuff is too easy to maintain. I like that Croak's area-effect attack doesn't deal TOO much damage, because it's very obvious what a difference medics make on a fight like this. that giant clump of Riflemen will survive with medics on the field! the boss has no way of thinning their numbers though, so the snowball is TOO great after it gets rolling. at that point it just ain't engaging ^^

since the boss is on the field for the entirety of this fight, it's kinda important that his attacks are fair. so here's an idea: his area-effect attacks can stay about the same. he already wombo-combos with the ladies' molotovs, or his son's bullet barrage... his SINGLE-target should be able to pick off lone riflemen, or deal substantial damage to more expensive infantry. that way, the player WILL need to spend units, will lose them over the course of the fight.
