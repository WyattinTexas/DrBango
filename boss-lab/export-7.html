Raziya's notes

a couple of good changes here! the flamers now survive snowballs from the second boss, so they're REALLY useful. the snowball cannons are cuter aesthetically, more endearing. and the second boss jumping around like it does prevents that exploit we discovered last time

however, there are some pretty glaring issues in other areas. boss HP isn't necessarily too high, but they take too long to kill because infantry durability is insufficient against certain attacks, and there aren't enough resources outside of the last wave to go nuts and have fun. the snowball cannons are simultaneously too good against regular enemies and too bad against bosses. if infantry could hold bosses to the right for longer, it would be both a more engaging and more fair fight, as it's possible the final boss especially just hops into the TB. speaking of hopping bosses though, the second one sometimes likes to hop so far up, he hides behind the health bar and we can't see when he's charging a snowball attack!

the infantry run rather zealously for outposts too, to the point they didn't attack a boss very close to the TB. this might not be so much of a problem if it were easier to keep the bosses further away from the TB :P besides adjusting that, should there, perhaps, be a cooldown on how soon the enemy can KO an Outpost after it's taken? getting one repeatedly stomped could be a potential point of frustration. ^^

I'll be sure to send some more details, and clips, in #balance-ops
