Raziya's notes

hmm. this fight seems to be the most fair to me so far, but also because I've been butting my head against harder stuff, it's also less engaging as a result XD

  • I like that there's a stampede during the pre-game. while I was still gathering myself and looking around, that prompted me to delay deploying troops. this is an important thing to telegraph, similarly to the camera pan I'm told is being looked at in the, uhh... King Yeti encounter
  • I was able to do nothing for quite a while since the boss hung out in the middle. send a first wave sooner? gotta make the player do SOMETHING XD there seem to be A LOT more enemies on the prior two fights compared to this one
  • this boss seems pretty fair because his area-effect attack does little enough damage that medics can do some work, but enough to blast away a good clump of riflemen eventually. that he seems to rely on single-target attacks for the most part means I can field all sorts of units and achieve victory. I like that aspect of it, mostly cuz I favor designs that present a variety of options for dealing with it. that said, it's pretty undertuned. being a frog, a high jump of some sort to attack or retreat wouldn't be out of place for this guy!
