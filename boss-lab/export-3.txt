NOTES: hmm...
 - the Outpost gimmick is fairly interesting, but there's currently no way to really dig yourself out of the hole if the enemy takes an Outpost. it seems that they have a slow firing rate with powerful attacks, which is good on OUR SIDE because it means the Outpost is still reliant on our forces to stay under our control, but really helps with taking out tough enemies and bosses. however, when on the enemy side, this means stronger infantry I'd LIKE to deploy to the field, like flamers or the sarge, seem like they'd be free real estate for this thing. the best solution then is to spam the cheapest unit and overwhelm the Outpost, provided they're promoted sufficiently to break past the accompanying enemies. Riflemen are already really strong and I'm inclined to use them quite often, so this seems like a bit of a problem for pigeonholing me even harder into them. FYI, I tried a Jeep, but it just drove past the Outpost. xD
 - perhaps adding at least one Outpost in the player's territory would be helpful for digging out of the hole and holding down the fort in a desperate situation. it can be along the road, so there's no cover nearby like the rocks around the other ones, and too far away to help the other two Outposts in an offensive position. but, it could be close enough to help the player regain control of other lost Outposts and help defend the other two Outposts.
 - the second wave's boss needs some reconsideration. besides having rather exploitable attacks, when its attacks AREN'T being exploited, it seems to crush all infantry equally. if non-Riflemen could survive a single hit, that would open up a lot more options.
 - third wave seems a lot more fair by comparison. the King Yeti has a wide slash attack that annihilates a large pack of infantry, but also seems to pause long enough between attacks and have enough other, less brutal attacks so my guys can do their work. a much more satisfying finish
