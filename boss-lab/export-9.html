Raziya's notes

since we're fighting the boss the whole time, similarly to the other Croak, there's that issue where we don't really have the opportunity to get a cash influx from defeating one boss after another like on King Yeti, or from toppling the boss once, like on Rex or Bear. on Croak, there were some rather unique enemies that could justifiably be made tough and given higher dogtag bounties... but on this fight, the princess has her armies of gnomes and bees and stuff. perhaps the answer then is to increase the amount of enemies being sent at the player, and not necessarily their value, on all difficulties.

that carries the risk of giving the enemy a critical mass of scary stuff though. for instance, a flamer can eat a hit from a small melee gnome, but not from two. if they show up in greater numbers, but deal single-target damage? they'll still be scary while rushing into the player's group of troops, there's more damage the player has to deal in order to defeat them (and this would make for good opportunities to use things like mortars, grenadiers and bazookas, the third thing especially if y'all like the idea that the armored gnomes have a similar weakness to rockets that vehicles have), and yet those numbers will also give the player more to work with to progress the encounter. I still think Princess Croak's harp could be some form of crowd control as opposed to an outright screen wipe, especially if she's consistently supported by other enemies that can push the player back during the stun

I'll go into some more details over in #balance-ops in a lil while. I got some clips to process.
