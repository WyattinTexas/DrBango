The one thing to know: in the Ice Castle almost every enemy hit is a KO, whatever a man's HP: the yetis' pounce, Sergeant Cold's big snowball and sprint, Sergeant Snowman's rolling ball on a light man, and every move King Yeti has (snowball, sword, seize, blue wind, leap). Only three hits take part of a man: the snowman's thrown ball (2 bar hits, MEGA and ULTRA MEGA ×1.2), THEIR turret since today (1.25 · 1.5 · 1.75 · 2.4 HP a round on EASY · MEDIUM · HARD · MEGA, a fifth on a heavy; ULTRA MEGA still a KO), and Sergeant Snowman's ball on a heavy (half his bar since today, MEGA 60 %; ULTRA MEGA a KO). Difficulty changes the bodies, the head-counts and the clocks; a KO hit is a KO on every tier. MEGA and ULTRA MEGA add +20 % to every bar hit, double the snow's slow, and bring the purple leaps.

How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP: the number on their unit sheet (the flame has 31 at level 25, the rifleman 14). Their health bar is also cut into a few bar hits (6 for most level-25 men, 5 for the medic and spy, 7 for the commando, 11 for the saber Sarge). Three kinds of hit in this fight:

  • KO hits (the pounce, the big snowballs, the sprint, the leaps, King Yeti's sword, seize and wind, Sergeant Snowman's ball on a light man, THEIR turret on ULTRA MEGA): out in one hit whatever his HP.
  • Bar hits (the snowman's thrown ball): 2 bar hits a ball, ×1.2 on MEGA and ULTRA MEGA, ×1.2 on the M. gunner. Since v28.65 a bar hit lands 90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP, so a level-25 rifleman takes 1.89 of his 6 (4.4 HP of 14). Sergeant Snowman's ball on a heavy is half his bar (bazooka · grenadier · flame · commando).
  • HP hits (THEIR turret, EASY to MEGA): a round of D takes D off his HP (1.25 · 1.5 · 1.75 · 2, MEGA ×1.2; a heavy ×0.2). A lone man takes all 3 rounds of a burst, a burst every 0.5 s.

A man who takes no hit for 4 s mends. Saves still roll on every hit: a jetting, dropping or dragged man cannot be touched, the pet shield, the deflect dome, the absorbs, and the saber Sarge's block turns a KO 50 % of the time at rank 25 (measured: 17, 16, 22, 18, 15 of 40 pounces on the five tiers). A charged commando catches a big snowball or a rolling ball and sends it back. The hero kings (King Yeti, King Rex) bounce every thrown and rolling snowball.
