How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP: the number on their unit sheet, the same in every mode (the flame has 55 at level 20, the rifleman 14). There are three kinds of hit in this fight:

  • HP hits (the sprinter, the pterodactyl, the triceratops, the bruiser, the dilophosaurus): a hit of D lands D × 0.5 (THE DAMPER, below) and takes that off his HP. A fresh man with that much HP or less is KO'd by one hit.
  • KO hits (the boulder, King Rex's bite): out in one hit whatever his HP.
  • Bar hits (the spitter's glob, the king's fire, his hop, his tail, the dilophosaurus on EASY): every man's health bar is also cut into a few bar hits (5 to 7 for a level-20 man, 8 at rank 100, more for the saber Sarge and the heroes). One bar hit is about a sixth of a level-20 man's HP: 2.2 HP of a rifleman's 14, 8.3 of a flame's 55. Since v28.65 a bar hit lands a little lighter on tough men (90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP), and since v29.17 at 50 % of that (below).

THE DAMPER (since v29.17, a row in the Boss Lab's RULES): every HP hit and bar hit an enemy lands on your men, Sarge, a hero or a vehicle in the campaign and the boss fights lands at 50 % (50 % off on every tier): a 15 HP bite lands 7.5, a bar hit 0.5 of one; a KO is still a KO. Every number in WHO SURVIVES and THE LEVEL A MAN FIRST SURVIVES counts it.

A man who takes no hit for 4 s mends. Saves still roll on every hit: a jetting or dragged man cannot be touched, a cloak hides the spy from the triceratops and the sprinter, the pet shield, the deflect dome, Sarge's and the Commander's absorbs, the saber's block and the dodges. A vehicle has 6 hits of hull: a dilophosaurus beat takes 0.5 of one and every other HP or KO strike one (a dent, as shipped), the hop and the tail sweep pass under it; the king's fire wrecks it in two beats (one breath) and a tail lash takes 5 of its 6.
