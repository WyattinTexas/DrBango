THE PILLOW FIELDS · EMPEROR BEAR · EVERY UNIT, EVERY DIFFICULTY

What every Snugg, cub, paratrooper, guard, Bluecoat, cannon, cavalryman and Emperor Bear does to your men on EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and MEGA, with every body's HP and head-count · read out of the live game v28.88 on Nov 1, 2026 03:48 EST · every damage number checked on the game's own machines (Chrome 329/329 · WebKit 329/329) · for Raziya

The one thing to know: in the Pillow Fields every blow of the bears' bodies is a KO, whatever a man's HP: the cub's pounce, the paratrooper's landing, a Bluecoat's sword, the hooves of the Emperor and his cavalry, and the Emperor's own sword (three cuts a lunge, three men down). Sarge is no exception here: under a moving horse any Sarge and Jane fall, and the Emperor's cut fells a saber Sarge without a block roll. Only three hits take part of a man: a Snugg's or a paratrooper's glob (2 bar hits, a man mends one for every 4 s left alone), a cotton cannon's lob (half his bar, so three lobs 3.5 s apart), and a Bluecoat's cut on Sarge or a hero (1 bar hit). Difficulty changes the bodies (×1 · 1.35 · 1.5 · 1.8), the head-counts (×1 · 1.25 · 1.5 · 1.5) and the lab's level; a hit is the same on every tier. MEGA brings nothing new to a hit: the +20 % and the double weather are the Ice Castle's law, not the fields'. The Emperor fights on two bars (152 · 233 · 287 · 390 each, solo at the lab's levels; the same price of the rung rides his Bluecoats, officer and cannons, and a party pays the tier alone: 289 · 390 · 433 · 520 with two commanders): one on the horse, one on foot.
THE BOSS LAB fights the Emperor with its stock army at LEVEL 50 on EASY, 57 on MEDIUM, 63 on HARD and 70 on MEGA: Sarge with the saber, the jeep, the tank, the dogfighter and the bomber, and seven men at that rank (rifleman, M. gunner, bazooka, mortar, medic, flame, sweeper). The radio man, the grenadier, the commando and the spy are a tap away in THE PLATOON. Every "level" number on this page is that army at that tier's level.

How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP: the number on their unit sheet (a level-50 flame has 61, a rifleman 26; at level 70: 80 and 33). Their health bar is also cut into a few bar hits (7 for most men at these levels, 6 for the medic and spy, 8 for the commando, 12 for the saber Sarge). Three kinds of hit in this fight:

  • KO hits (the cub's pounce, the paratrooper's landing, a Bluecoat's cut on a man, the hooves, the Emperor's sword and saddle swing): out in one hit whatever his HP.
  • Bar hits (a glob: 2; a Bluecoat's cut on Sarge or a hero: 1; the Emperor's or the cavalry's blow on a hero: 1): since v28.65 a bar hit lands 90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP, so a level-50 rifleman takes 1.85 of his 7 from a glob (6.9 HP of 26). A man who takes no hit for 4 s mends one bar hit, and again every 4 s.
  • The cotton lob: half the man's bar × that share, so 3.24 of a level-50 rifleman's 7: three lobs 3.5 s apart KO every man (two KO a hero, whose share is 1); the jeep and the tank take one hull hit a lob.

Saves still roll on every hit: a jetting, dropping or dragged man cannot be touched, the pet shield, the deflect dome, the absorbs, and the saber Sarge's block turns a glob, a lob, a pounce or a Bluecoat's cut 50 % of the time (measured: 27, 19, 23, 16 of 40 pounces turned on the four tiers on Chrome, 14, 19, 18, 28 on WebKit: 14–28 of 40, a coin's spread about 20). It never turns the Emperor's cut or a moving horse: those come already rolled (measured 10 KO of 10 cuts, 0 blocks; every Sarge and Jane under the hooves on every tier). The Claymore Sarge's counted block and the riot plate keep their say. A Bluecoat whose man is a saber Sarge mid-charge waits for the strike to land, then cuts.
