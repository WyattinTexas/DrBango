KING CROAK'S HIDEOUT · EVERY UNIT, EVERY DIFFICULTY

What King Croak, his son, the posse, the ladies, the rattlesnake and the stampede do to your men on EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and MEGA, with every body's HP and head-count · read out of the live game v28.88 on Oct 31, 2026 22:19 · every damage number checked on the game's own machines (Chrome 97/97 · WebKit 97/97) · for Raziya

The one thing to know: every hit in the hideout is a bar hit (the King Rex page's word): it takes a share of a man's health bar, not a fixed HP. King Croak's and his son's hits grow with difficulty: × 1 · 1.1 · 1.1 · 1.2 (EASY · MEDIUM · HARD · MEGA). On MEGA (CARD-MKC) everything on his side also hits twice as hard and has six times the bar, so his strong shot is 4.8 bar hits against 2 on EASY. The bandits, sharpshooters, ladies and rattlesnake hit the same on EASY, MEDIUM and HARD. The stampede and the ladies' fire never change. Only the snake's swallow is a straight KO.
THE BOSS LAB fights King Croak with its stock army: Sarge with the saber, the jeep, the plane and 9 men (rifleman, M. gunner, bazooka, mortar, medic, flame, sweeper, radio man, grenadier), at level 30 on EASY, 33 on MEDIUM, 37 on HARD and 40 on MEGA (MEGA adds the tank and leaves the grenadier home). At the lab's levels 30, 33 and 37 the stock army reads the same HP and bar, so EASY, MEDIUM and HARD meet the same men (not every rank between: the stock Sarge's saber reads 29 HP at ranks 38 and 39 against 28 at 30–37; the Claymore Sarge and the heroes climb a little); MEGA's level-40 men are one step tougher. The lab fields no hero; the hero rows below show one in command at the same level.

How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP (the number on their unit sheet) and a health bar cut into a few bar hits: 5 to 7 for a level-30 man, 7 to 9 at rank 100, 11 for the saber Sarge and 22 with the Claymore, 43 to 101 for a hero. A man is KO'd when the bar hits he has taken fill his bar.

  • Every attack in the hideout carries a number of bar hits (the tables below). Since v28.65 a man takes 90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP of each: 0.933 for a level-30 rifleman, 0.915 for a flame. Heroes take them whole.
  • In HP, one bar hit lands as that man's HP ÷ his bar × his share: 2.8 HP of a level-30 rifleman's 18, 6.25 of a flame's 41. The HP figures on this page are that conversion.
  • A man who takes no hit for 4 s mends. Saves still roll on every hit: a jetting or dragged man cannot be touched, the pet shield, the deflect dome, Sarge's and the Commander's absorbs, the saber's block (one in two from rank 20) and the dodges. A man inside the snake's mouth is gone whatever his bar.
