THE ICE CAVES · THE FEROCIOUS YETI · EVERY UNIT, EVERY DIFFICULTY

What every cave yeti, spearman, fisher, dark yeti swordsman and THE FEROCIOUS YETI does to your men on EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and MEGA, with every body's HP and head-count · read out of the live game v29.00 on Nov 1, 2026 16:01 EST · every damage number checked on the game's own machines (Chrome 381/381 · WebKit 381/381) · for Raziya

The one thing to know: in the caves three blows kill a foot soldier whatever his HP: a swordsman's cut, THE FEROCIOUS YETI's landing (THE CHOMP, 58 px wide) and the falling ice (which kills everyone under it: Sarge of every iron, the jeep, the tank, every hero). Every other blow is bar hits off the one bar every man carries (7 for the men, 6 for the medic, 12 for the saber Sarge) × the share: a cave yeti's landing, a spearman's charge, a fisher's fish and the stomp 1 · 1 · 2 · 2 by tier, the chase's run 2, a cut on Sarge or the medic 3, THE CHOMP on them 2 · 3 · 4 · 4. So the heavies are not heavy here: a flame (51 HP) and a rifleman (22 HP) fall to the same cut, the same landing, the same shard, and stand the same 7 landings. King Yeti and King Rex are the exception the other way: every cave creature's blow bounces off their hide (measured: nothing lands but the ice). Difficulty changes the bar hits (1 → 2 from HARD), THE CHOMP (2 · 3 · 4 · 4), the bodies (×1 · 1.5 · 2.2 · 2.64), the head-counts, his bar (1,080 · 1,890 · 1,881 · 3,089) and the lab's level.
THE BOSS LAB fights the caves with its stock army at LEVEL 45 on EASY, 47 on MEDIUM, 48 on HARD and 50 on MEGA: Sarge with the saber, the jeep, the tank and the dogfighter (the bomber on MEGA), the jet pack on, and eight men at that rank (rifleman, M. gunner, bazooka, mortar, medic, flame, sweeper, radio man; MEGA swaps the radio man for the bomber). The grenadier, the commando and the spy are a tap away in THE PLATOON. Every "level" number on this page is that army at that tier's level. At levels 45 to 48 the men's HP is the same number (the ladder steps every tenth level); 50 is a step up.

How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP: the number on their unit sheet (a level-45 flame has 51, a rifleman 22; at level 50: 61 and 26). Their health bar is also cut into a few bar hits (7 for most men at these levels, 6 for the medic and the spy, 8 for the commando, 12 for the saber Sarge, 23 for the Claymore Sarge). Two kinds of blow in this fight:

  • Kill blows (a swordsman's cut and THE CHOMP on a foot soldier: rifleman, M. gunner, bazooka, mortar, grenadier, sweeper, flame, radio man, commando, spy; the falling ice on anyone): out in one, whatever his HP. Only a wall turns them: a pet's shield, Francis's block, the commando's roof, the blue wall or the dome turns the blow into bar hits (1 · 1 · 2 · 2 for the cut, 3 for a shard); the champion's grace once a life.
  • Bar hits (a landing, a charge, a fish, the stomp: 1 · 1 · 2 · 2; the run: 2; a cut on Sarge, the medic, a machine or a hero: 3; THE CHOMP on them: 2 · 3 · 4 · 4): since v28.65 a bar hit lands 90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP, so a level-45 rifleman takes 0.93 of his 7 from a cave yeti's landing (2.9 HP of 22). A hero's share is 1. A man who takes no hit for 4 s mends one bar hit, and again every 4 s.

Saves still roll on every bar hit: a jetting, dropping or dragged man cannot be touched, the pet shield, the deflect dome, the absorbs, and the saber Sarge's block turns a landing, a charge, a fish, a cut or THE CHOMP about half the time (measured: 4–7 of 10 landings, 2–8 of 10 cuts and 2–5 of 10 chomps turned over the four tiers and both engines). King Yeti's and King Rex's hide turns every blow of a cave creature (a thrown-snow law: the whole cave family counts as snow), the falling ice alone takes them. The Claymore Sarge's counted block and the riot plate keep their say. Nothing turns the ice but a shield.
