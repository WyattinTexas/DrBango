PRINCESS CROAK'S PALACE · PRINCESS CROAK · EVERY UNIT, EVERY DIFFICULTY

What every gnome, fairy, wasp, frog and Princess Croak herself does to your men on EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and MEGA, with every body's HP and head-count · read out of the live game v28.88 on Nov 1, 2026 04:21 EST · every damage number checked on the game's own machines (Chrome 244/244 · WebKit 244/244) · for Raziya

The one thing to know: in the palace almost every hit takes BAR HITS off a man, not a KO: her harp shot (2 bar hits, MEGA 3), the guard's jab (1), the knight's jab (2), the slinger's glob (1), the wasp's sting (1, and the man is held 2 s), the hornet's sting (3, and he is bowled over), the frog's splat and tongue (1 each), and the sage's fire (1 a beat, ten beats). A level-70 rifleman has 7 bar hits (33 HP), so her harp KOs him on the 4th shot on EASY to HARD and the 3rd on MEGA. Three things are a KO whatever a man's HP: the gnome guard's RAM (a charge at a man ahead within 300 px; a jeep or the tank is destroyed on the spear; the saber turns it about half the time, THE RIOT SHIELD's 5 and the Claymore's counted block eat it), her LIGHT ARROWS (MEDIUM up, at half her bar; MEGA again at a quarter: every man a bolt crosses, through every save: the one hit nothing turns), her FACE SHOT (a Sarge within 84 px: through the saber's block; the riot shield and the Claymore's block eat it, the deflect dome turns it) — and the sage's toad curse takes Sarge out of the fight for 5 s. Difficulty changes her bar (×2 · ×2.4 · ×6 · ×9), her lives (1 · 1 · 3 · 3), her cadence, the court's size and HP, and brings the arrows, the knights, the sages and the hornets.
THE BOSS LAB fights the palace at LEVEL 70 · 77 · 83 · 90 (EASY · MEDIUM · HARD · MEGA): Sarge with the saber, the scout jeep, the dogfighter, the tank, the bomber and seven men at that rank (rifleman, M. gunner, bazooka, mortar, medic, flame, sweeper). The radio man, grenadier, commando and spy are a tap away in THE PLATOON. Sarge wears the two rank-5 gifts (telekinesis on the saber; the riot shield answers only with the shotgun in hand). Every "level" number on this page is that army.

How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP: the number on their unit sheet (a level-70 flame has 80, a rifleman 33). Their health bar is also cut into a few bar hits (7 for most men at level 70–90, 6 for the medic and spy, 8 for the commando, 12 for the saber Sarge, 23 for the Claymore). Three kinds of hit in this fight:

  • Bar hits (everything the court does, and her harp): a hit of N bar hits lands 90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP of N since v28.65, so a level-70 rifleman takes 1.84 of his 7 from her harp (8.7 HP of 33) and 0.92 from a guard's jab. Bar hits mend after 4 s without a hit. A hero's bar IS his HP pool (a bar hit lands whole on it).
  • KO hits (the guard's ram, her light arrows, her face shot): out in one hit whatever his HP. Only the arrows go through every save. Her face shot goes through the saber's block (the shot is sent with the saber's roll switched off: the KO door's rolled flag, by design, the auto-kill for a Sarge who comes too close), but THE RIOT SHIELD (5 a battle, the shotgun) and the Claymore's counted block (1 a battle: THE DUELIST, every Claymore's default spec; three only with FEAR or WALL picked) eat it whole, and the deflect dome turns it: measured, the shotgun Sarge fell to the 6th face shot and the Claymore to the 2nd. The ram reaches that door with no roll spent, so every save answers it: the saber turns it about half the time (measured 6/10 · 7/8 · 6/3 · 9/11 of 16 fresh Sarges KO'd on E · M · H · G, Chrome/WebKit), the plate and the counted block eat it (the shotgun Sarge fell to the 6th ram, the Claymore to the 2nd), the pet shield, the blue wall and the teal turn it; the deflect dome does not (its KO door catches only the officer's potshot and her face shot).
  • No hit: the fairy mends her court and bubbles a guard BIG; the bubble itself pops on a green round; the sage's curse turns Sarge into a toad for 5 s (no fire, no swing).

Saves still roll on every bar hit: a jetting, dropping or dragged man cannot be touched, the pet shield, the deflect dome, the saber Sarge's block turns a bar hit 50 % of the time (measured: 6 of 15, 17 of 29, 4 of 11, 3 of 8 of her shots on the four tiers), the shotgun Sarge's riot shield eats the first 5 hits whole (measured), and the Claymore's counted block eats 1 (measured; THE DUELIST's one block is every Claymore's default spec, the table's 3 only with FEAR or WALL picked). The same saves answer the guard's ram, all but the deflect dome (its KO door catches only the officer's potshot and her face shot); her face shot skips only the saber's roll; her light arrows skip them all. A charged commando catches a light arrow and sends it back at her.
