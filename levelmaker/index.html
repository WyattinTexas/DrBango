A level is a list of waves on a piece of ground. A wave is who walks on, in what shape, from which edge, at what moment; the ground is its size and what it's made of — snow, western sand, or the crimson rug, at the regular size or the battleground's big span. Tap any level and change those things with your thumb — the picture redraws from the game's own arithmetic every time, so what you see here is exactly what marches in the game.

How this tool stays honest.
The page carries the game's real campaign code — levelCfg() and the shipped CAMP_EDITS overlay, lifted verbatim from index.html — and re-runs it with your changes on every tap. Every picture and every count on this page is that code's own output. The tool never touches the game itself: it hands you one block, and a person commits it.
