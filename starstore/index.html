The Menu Sky the 420 by 800 meadow frame, painted with SKY-DESIGN's dusk

The imagery board. The showcase seat at y 150 cycles the upgraded figures every nine seconds the way the home cycler does (lite aura, no halo); Venus stands steady over the horizon glow; a slow comet crosses; the wordmark and buttons are drawn placeholders so the whole reads as the real menu.

  • The dusk is the game's own skygrad stops, baked 64 by 1024 with the scanline dither, shown as the home frame (world 1600 to 2400): the plum at the top and the horizon gold at 55 percent are the shipped colours.
  • The showcase seat sits at design (0,150), assembled at 0.8 with the lite aura (alpha 0.07, no halo, glints and breath still running). Its box, x 100 to 320 and y 70 to 230, is the keep-out for decor.
  • Venus at (335,392): the spark4 cross at 1.35, alpha 0.85, a glowbig halo at 0.12; it never twinkles, which is the tell that it is a world.
  • The moon keeps its seat at frame (70,425), 104 units, tilted 24 degrees, the shipped crescent by default; dial D13 swaps in tonight's true phase.
  • The comet crosses from (-40,120) to (460,50) below the showcase, tail pointing away from the sunset corner, on the R8 clock.
  • The wish is one pooled streak from the radiant at (380,20), cream, mint or ember, on the R9 clock; tap it in the game to wish, here the button.
  • Fireflies, the two hills and the grass strip are set dressing from SKY-DESIGN section 4.
