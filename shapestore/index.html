❓ HOW THE SHOP WORKS

Just draw: press ✏️ DRAW and scribble — a big fat pencil in whatever paint chip you tapped last. Three dot sizes. 👆 MOVE goes back to grabbing things.
Start from a real GVT toy: tap any FINISHED CHARACTER — it's the actual mold from the game. Drag it, resize with the corner handle, spin with the top handle.
Coloring: select a toy and its colors appear as swatches on the right. Tap a swatch, then tap any paint chip at the bottom (or the 🎨 wheel) — every piece wearing that color changes at once, just like the game recolors its toys.
Build your own: SHAPES are bodies, spikes, limbs, stars, eyes… they paint with whatever chip you tapped last.
Keep your own shapes: select anything you made (or drew) and press ⭐ KEEP — it joins MY SHAPES on the left so you can stamp it out again anytime, like your own decoration shelf.
Pick one piece: double-tap a toy to grab a single piece — move it, recolor it, or delete it (take the crown off, move the head…).
Scenes: switch to PARK SCENE for a wide 1280×640 park hero board with skies, volcanoes, pines and bone gates.
Saving: 💾 SAVE keeps it in the GALLERY on this device. EXPORT → JSON makes a file you can text to each other and IMPORT on the other computer.
Keys: arrows nudge · Del delete · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+D duplicate · [/] send back / bring forward.

WYATT'S ART LAWS (house style)

· No smiles on the wrong faces · A skull needs a CHIN · Cute = round head, big eye, rosy cheek, belly panel · Nothing floats above the horizon fold · Paths trail off, never tangle · Thick round-capped strokes read as molded plastic.
