From Laura, 11/4/2026: "make this like our last robot, mark as finished and send to jumpr to put in the game." The character still used the old robot. He now has the same drawing as the Robo-Sarge 3000 treasure, everywhere he shows. It is live in client v30.18.

Why he was left behind: the game copied the toy's drawing for the character while it loads, before your treasures swap in. So the fights, the level banner, the next-up card, the boss wall, the Log and the rescue scene kept the old robot while the toy on the rug was already yours.

The switch

On one device, ?rbc=0 at the end of the game's address shows the robot as he was before this card, and ?rbc=1 brings yours back. The treasure keeps its own switch (?ltr=0). Nothing else in the game changed: no number of his fights, no word, no save.
