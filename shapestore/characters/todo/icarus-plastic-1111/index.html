THE SIX QUESTIONS

A letter answers each.

ICR-Q1

1 · THE LOOK

AONE MOLD. Every part the one black plastic, as an army man is all one green. Only his eye is paint.

BONE MOLD, THE CHEST IN GOLD. The same black mold with one paint: the scales of his chest.

CTHE PAINTED TOY. Black plastic with today's colours painted on: ember chest and back plates, red wings, ivory horns, teeth and claws.

MY PICK: B. It is the army men's plastic from nose to tail, and the chest is where the eye goes, which is what you asked the chest to be. It also finds him in a fight: on the battle rug's crimson this black reads 1.2 to 1 (the game's own note gives a black army 1.3 to 1), so look A nearly disappears at fighting size. C is the closest to how he is drawn today and the furthest from an army man.

ICR-Q2

2 · HIS NAME ON THE GLASS

AICARUS, alone, everywhere.

BICARUS, with THE BLACK DRAGON under it. The stamp reads ICARUS, THE BLACK DRAGON, JOINS YOUR ARMY.

CTHE BLACK DRAGON stays the title. Icarus is what he is called when someone speaks to him.

A hero sells by his name (KING REX, EMPEROR BEAR), and THE BLACK DRAGON under it tells a new player what Icarus is and keeps the words the promotion, the store sheet and the film already use. Six letters also write bigger and faster across a phone than sixteen. Your 11/11 note on the film made Sarge's line "Please welcome our newest hero!": whether he then says the name is yours.

AYes. While he draws breath the scales go hot, and the plate glows until the fire stops.

ICR-Q5

AOn the ground he stands on an army man's oval stand in his own plastic. In the air he is drawn without it.

MY PICK: A. The stand is the plainest sign in the game that a figure is a plastic army toy. No toy flies with its stand.

HOW IT WAS CHECKED

  • Every drawing was drawn twice, in Chrome and in WebKit (the iPhone's engine), and compared: they agree (39.8 to 45.2 dB, no tone moved by more than a tenth of a level).
  • No drawing uses a filter. A filter is what makes art read stronger on an iPhone than on the page it was approved on, and it is what costs a phone memory.
  • The men beside him are the game's own drawings, pulled out of the running game (client v31.54), never copied by hand.
  • Zero game bytes: nothing in the game, on a site or in a store changed.
