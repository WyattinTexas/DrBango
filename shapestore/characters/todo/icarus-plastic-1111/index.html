THE LOOK

THE FIRE, FIXED

Wyatt: "Get the fire breathing to come out more accurately." It used to begin in front of his face. Now it starts in his throat, fills the open mouth between the teeth, and leaves along the mouth's own line, in every pose.

WHAT MAKES HIM READ AS THE ARMY MEN'S PLASTIC

So a tweak keeps it. These six are how the game draws its army men today:

  1. One plastic colour under everything.
  2. A lighter band on the side that faces the light (top left).
  3. A darker edge on the side away from it.
  4. A white gloss band along the top of a limb.
  5. A fine speckle.
  6. The oval stand: a lit top, a shaded front.
