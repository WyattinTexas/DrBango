Every character in Green vs Tan

142 characters, 271 drawings (every pose each one has in the game), straight out of the game, version 18.90. Each one is a 2048 by 2048 JPEG on white.

This page keeps itself up to date. When a new version of the game goes live, every drawing here is redrawn from it, usually within half an hour (now: version 18.90, October 9 at 9:17 PM Eastern). Numbers never change; a new character gets the next free number. A drawing that changed since the first list says UPDATED IN and the version. 31 drawings say so now.

Layers for Procreate: tap LAYERS (PSD) under a drawing. Your browser builds a layered Photoshop file of it (every coloured piece its own layer, the drawing's own parts as groups, 48 layers at most) and saves it to Files, Downloads. In Procreate: Gallery, Import, Downloads, the file. The number on the button is its layer count.
Just one? Tap its picture to open the full JPEG, then press and hold it and choose Save to Photos (or Share, Procreate). In Procreate: Gallery, Import (from Files) or Photo (from Photos). A letter after the number (001-a, 001-b) is the same character in another pose.
