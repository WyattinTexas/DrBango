One page for all the art: six sections down the page, every picture numbered and straight out of the game, with Al's and Laura's marks. How it works The page opens on what is Finished: everything either of you has ticked Finished, from every section, the newest first, with LAURA, AL or LAURA + AL for whose; and on the filter bar, Show: NEW | OLD turns every finished piece on the page to its new drawing or its old one. Then come the characters, then the treasures, the buttons and menus, the gameplay and the screens, one section after another, every picture straight out of the game. The buttons at the top jump to each section; the one you are in is lit. Ticks for Al and Laura: tap Working on it on anything when you start on it, and Finished when you are happy with its final art. You both see the same marks, each with who set it and when; they refresh by themselves every 20 seconds. Tap your own lit mark again to take it off. A mark the other person set asks first before it becomes yours. Pick your name at the top once on each device. The counts and the filters at the top are the whole page's: Working shows everything either of you is working on, in every section, and a section with nothing under the filter folds away. The number on each section's button is what it shows under the filter. Every picture has a number that never changes: the characters 001 on, then V for the villages, BG for the background characters, T for the treasures, B for the buttons and menus, G for the gameplay and S for the screens.

Characters

179 characters, 328 drawings (every pose each one has in the game), straight out of the game, version 28.58. Each one is a 2048 by 2048 JPEG on white. At the end of the page: the villages, 43 Shape Store works, each with the file the Shape Store opens. After them, the background characters (46), each with its JPEG and its layers (jump to them). The treasures have their own section further down.

Shaded like Sarge's hats. 300 drawings here wear the hats' shading (Al's ask, October 11): each coloured piece keeps its colour and its outline, with a lighter edge of the same colour on its upper left and a darker band low on its right. Your own drawings and the army men wear it too; Jane stays as you drew her. The outer outlines are thinner and every line takes its own piece's colour, a step darker, the way Bluey is drawn (Al's rule, October 11): a pale piece gets a soft line, a dark piece a nearly black one, a line over another piece keeps its own colour, and a smile or a toe line takes the colour of the piece it sits on. Every line is even and a little heavier than Bluey's (Al's pick of three), and a character drawn without an outline gets one the same way (Al, October 13). The army men wear it too, with a heavier, darker line of their own and lighter shading kept to the edges that face the background, so each man reads as one form (Al, October 13). Every new character gets it the moment it appears here, and the game draws them the same way (since October 15, Al's ask).

This page keeps itself up to date. When a new version of the game goes live, every drawing here is redrawn from it, usually within half an hour (now: version 28.58, October 30 at 5:53 PM Eastern). Numbers never change; a new character gets the next free number and says NEW IN and the version (the Masters' stages' animals are at the end, one group per continent). A drawing that changed since the first list says UPDATED IN and the version. 311 drawings say so now.

Layers for Procreate: tap LAYERS (PSD) under a drawing. Your browser builds a layered Photoshop file of it (every coloured piece its own layer, the drawing's own parts as groups, 48 layers at most) and saves it to Files, Downloads. In Procreate: Gallery, Import, Downloads, the file. The number on the button is its layer count.
Just one? Tap its picture to open the full JPEG, then press and hold it and choose Save to Photos (or Share, Procreate). In Procreate: Gallery, Import (from Files) or Photo (from Photos). A letter after the number (001-a, 001-b) is the same character in another pose.
The villages: every house, shop, prop and animal at the end of this page is a Shape Store work. Tap SHAPE STORE FILE under one to save its file, then on the Shape Store's desk (drbango.com/shapestore) tap GALLERY, then IMPORT A .JSON WORK, and pick it: every piece is yours to move or recolour. Tap its picture for the JPEG.
