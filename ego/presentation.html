01 — The Concept

Your personality,
running as software.

A Digital Ego captures who you are — how you think, how you talk, what you know, what you value — and runs it as a conversational AI clone. Ask it anything. It answers as you.

1

Map the personality

We extract identity, communication patterns, values, decision-making frameworks, current life state, and deep knowledge domains into a structured personality profile.

2

Calibrate the voice

Few-shot example exchanges train the AI on exactly how you'd respond — your sentence length, your humor, your pushback, your silence. Not what you'd say. How you'd say it.

3

Run it live

A single HTML file. No server. No backend. The Claude API processes your personality DNA and responds in real time, streaming your voice back through the browser.

02 — The Personality Layer

Six strands of DNA.

A digital ego isn't a chatbot wearing a mask. It's built from layers of real personality data — each one shaping how the AI thinks, speaks, and decides.

🧬

Identity & Background Core

Who you are. Age, location, role, history. The facts that shape your perspective on everything else.

"35, Estero FL. Solo founder. Game designer since I was a kid. First card I ever made was called Crab."
🗣

Communication Style Voice

How you talk. Sentence length. Humor type. What you'd never say. The texture of your language.

Short. Direct. Lead with the answer. No filler. No "well, I think..." — just the take.
🧭

Values & Decision Framework Compass

Your principles. What you optimize for. How you make calls under pressure. The rules you live by.

"Disney World quality bar. Not 'does it work' — does it light up their eyes?"
⚡

Current Life State Live

What you're working on right now. Active projects, deadlines, priorities. This decays fast — it's the most human layer.

"Polishing multiplayer on mobile for the booth Saturday. Then hard pivot to ad production."
📚

Knowledge Domains Brain

What you know deeply. Card game balance theory. Manufacturing logistics. Dice math. Pickleball mechanics. Retro games.

"HP is king in a 1-3 damage system. Every HP point = 1-3 extra winning rolls to survive."
🚧

Behavioral Rules Guardrails

Response constraints. What the ego must always do, and what it must never do. The fence that keeps the voice authentic.

Never hedge. Never use corporate speak. Never say "that's impossible." Have opinions. Share them.
"We are going to try to bring love with what we do."
— Wyatt Gable, encoded into his Digital Ego
